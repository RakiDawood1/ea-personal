Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

EA Personal is a private tool. Only its owner, Rakhan Dawood, uses it. It only accesses his own Google account.

What it reads

  • Gmail: it reads emails in the inbox. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar: it reads events and can add events at the owner's request.

What it does with that data

It turns the data into a short summary and sends it to the owner's own Telegram chat. To write the summary, the email text is processed by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The tool does not save emails to its own files. It does not sell, share or publish the data.
